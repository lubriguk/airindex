02 / Arrival cause index

NAS-attributed minutes

ORIGIN-BASED / BTS ATTRIBUTION

Arrival-delay cause minutes reported for flights originating at each airport. Not a measure of departure delay or proof of airport responsibility.

Reading the rankings. Delayed departures are flights leaving 15 minutes or more after schedule, divided by reported departure flights. NAS is one of five BTS-attributed arrival-delay causes for flights originating at each airport. Per-arrival uses flights with reported arrival times; share uses the sum of all five attributed cause-minute fields. N/A means no denominator was reported. Attribution does not establish where or why an airport was responsible.

Source: Bureau of Transportation Statistics
